# DropBeam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

DropBeam is a peer-to-peer file transfer and chat app for Mac, Windows, Linux and iPhone. It is built so that your files and messages travel directly between your own devices and the friends you choose, and so that we hold as little of your data as possible.

## What DropBeam does with your data

- **Files and messages go device to device.** Transfers and chat messages are sent over an end-to-end encrypted connection directly between your device and the recipient's device. When a direct connection is not possible, traffic passes through a relay server that only forwards encrypted bytes and cannot read them.
- **No account with us.** DropBeam does not ask you to create an account. Your identity in the app is a cryptographic key generated on your device. Your friends list, chat history and settings are stored on your device only.
- **Linking your own devices.** If you link a phone or another computer to your account, your friends list and chat history are copied directly from one of your devices to the other over the same encrypted connection. Nothing is uploaded to us.
- **Photos and files you choose.** On iPhone, DropBeam only receives the specific photos or files you pick in the system picker. It does not read your photo library.
- **Local network.** DropBeam uses your local network to find your own devices and friends nearby so transfers can go directly. It does not scan or store information about other devices.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code to add a friend or link a device. No images are stored.

## Diagnostics

DropBeam can send a small anonymous diagnostics digest so we can find bugs and slowness. It contains error and performance lines from the app's own log. Before anything is sent, file names, paths, device identifiers, network addresses and email addresses are removed. Diagnostics can be turned off at any time in Settings.

If you send feedback from inside the app, the message, an optional screenshot you review first, and basic device information (OS version, app version, device model) are sent to us so we can respond. Crash reports may be included so we can fix crashes.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not use advertising or tracking identifiers.
- We do not store your files, messages, contacts or photos on our servers.

## Data on your device

You can delete everything DropBeam stores by removing a friend, clearing history, or uninstalling the app.
